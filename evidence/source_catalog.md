# Source Catalog

All URLs were checked on **2026-11-14**. NASA Human Research Program (HRP) report URLs were
verified by downloading the PDF and extracting text. Where a source could not be retrieved, it is
listed under "Sources not used" rather than cited as evidence.

**Transparency note:** NASA standards and HRP evidence reports were downloaded and their relevant
sections read directly. Some peer-reviewed entries (for example Belenky 2003, Van Dongen 2003,
Dawson & Reid 1997, Basner 2011/2012, Marshall-Goebel 2019, Lee 2015, Meck 2001, and Law 2014)
were used through the NASA report or technical brief that cites them, not as independently
downloaded full texts. Those records are still traceable to the NASA source that summarizes them.
Where a fact depends on an independently read abstract or full text, the DOI or PMID is given in the
registry record.

Counts: 33 agency and peer-reviewed entries (31 cited by a record or used as a cross-check; OSDR and
NLSP/LSDA were reviewed as potential data sources only), plus 7 sources listed as not used.

## Access notes

- NASA standards and HRP evidence reports are public.
- LSAH and Standard Measures describe public programs, but the underlying individual health data are
  not public. This registry does not claim access to them.
- OSDR is described by NASA as open access for biological and physical science datasets. It is not a
  substitute for clinical monitoring data.
- NLSP/LSDA is a portal; dataset-level access terms must be checked before use.
- The Geneva et al. DOI resolves in a browser but returns HTTP 403 to automated non-browser
  requests; the article abstract and PubMed record were used, not the publisher HTML.
